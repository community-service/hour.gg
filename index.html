Community Service Hour

Bring the work. Get it argued with, live.

Every Tuesday, one hour. Bring a change and take the floor, or listen while someone else does. The reaction is live: design, implementation, and whether it is correct.

Join the Next Live Call Listen to Recent Episodes

This is for you if…

You can produce a draft, and still cannot tell if it is right. A model will write the code. It will not defend the design on a live call.

You do not yet know a correct change from a wrong one. A first pull request is a rep. It is not a portfolio.

You would rather watch the argument than read a tutorial. The episodes are the worked example.

How it works

1
Make a change

A typo fix, a small pull request, or the thing you are building. Bring a diff.

2
Show up, or listen

One hour, live. In the ring, the work goes on the table. In the audience, you watch that hour.

3
Get it argued with

Design, implementation, and whether it is correct. Early is fine. Unchecked is not.

4
Leave a record

The episode, the notes, and the participant page are the proof it was looked at.

Never landed a correct change before? Start there.

Create a GitHub profile. Pick a project you can actually read, including this site. Make the smallest correct change and open a pull request. Then bring the diff to the call. You will get a reaction, not a medal for having a profile.
